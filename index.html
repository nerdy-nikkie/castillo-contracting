About

I'm Nico Castillo, and I've been doing handyman and repair work since 2014. In that time I've handled electrical, plumbing and general home repairs for homeowners, renters and property managers — appliances, cabinets, doors, locks, faucets and pipes. I carry all my own tools and equipment, so I show up ready to work.

I work as an independent contractor, which means you deal with me directly from the first call to the last walkthrough. No call center, no rotating crew, no surprise handoff. That is how I have kept a 5.0 rating across 47 verified reviews.

I am also a trained engineer and 3D modeler, which earns its keep on the jobs that have no off-the-shelf fix. When a part is discontinued or a repair needs something custom, I can design and make it instead of telling you it cannot be done.
